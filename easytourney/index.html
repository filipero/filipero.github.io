Everything you need to run a tournament

⚡

Instant brackets

Pick up to 64 players with names, colors, and emojis — your single-elimination bracket builds itself, seeding and BYEs included.

📲

Friends join live

Open a lobby and share a QR code or link. Friends join from their own iPhones, and the roster fills in as they arrive.

👥

Team tournaments

Doubles, trios, 5-a-side, or your own squad size — the app assembles the teams and you can rename them before kickoff.

👀

Watch mode

Anyone with the link can follow the bracket live as results come in — perfect for players knocked out early and fans on the sideline.

🏆

Scores & notes

Tap a match to record the winner and score, and add notes like "won on penalties." The bracket advances automatically.

🥉

Third-place match

Turn on the bronze final so the semifinal losers still get to battle for the podium.

📺

Presentation mode

A full-screen, read-only bracket made for an iPad propped up at the venue — and the screen never sleeps.

🖼️

Share the glory

Export the bracket or a podium result card, revisit past champions in the Hall of Fame, and rematch with one swipe.

How it works

Create

Choose singles or teams, then add players yourself or open a live lobby and let friends join with a QR code.

Play

Tap matches to record winners and scores. Everyone watching sees the bracket update in real time.

Crown a champion

Share the podium card, save the champion to your Hall of Fame, and run it back with a one-swipe rematch.
